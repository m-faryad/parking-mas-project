# Viva practice questions (for you to answer in your own words)

1. Why an agent-based model rather than a purely statistical queueing model?
2. Which parts of the system are genuinely agents? Which behaviour is centralised?
3. What does the baseline ignore? Why is it a fair comparator?
4. Explain the coordinated cost function. Where do the weights come from and how would you justify or tune them?
5. Why does the coordinated strategy increase drive distance and the emission proxy in these results? What does that say about your objective?
6. What is the unit/denominator of success rate? What happens to pending requests?
7. How are seeds used so the comparison is paired? What would break pairing?
8. What does a 95% interval that spans zero tell you here? Why Holm correction?
9. Why is the emission proxy not a measured environmental benefit? What would be needed to claim one?
10. How do you know the simulation never double-allocates a space? Which tests show it?
11. What are the threats to validity of using synthetic data?
12. What would you change first with another month?
13. Which parts of the implementation did you write, and which did tools generate? Be able to explain every module.
